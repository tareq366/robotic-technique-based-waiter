# Design and Implementation of A Robotic Technique Based Waiter

## About the Research

This repository presents my published research work titled **"Design and Implementation of A Robotic Technique Based Waiter."**

The research focuses on the design and implementation of a robotic waiter system designed to assist restaurant operations through robotics and automation.

## Research Areas

- Robotics and Automation
- Service Robotics
- Intelligent Systems
- Human-Robot Interaction
